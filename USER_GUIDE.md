# Public Donor Guide - Donation.app

Welcome to **Donation.app**! This platform provides a zero-friction, transparent way to support **Ganesh Chaturthi** and **Dasara** festival celebrations.

---

## 🚀 How to Contribute (Under 1 Minute)

### Step 1: Browse Festival Events
Visit the home page [https://donation.app](https://donation.app) and select either **🕉️ Ganesh Chaturthi** or **🏹 Dasara**. No registration or account creation is required!

### Step 2: View Progress & Click "Donate Now"
Check the committee's live progress bar (Target, Collected, Remaining) and click **❤️ Donate Now**.

### Step 3: Fill Minimal Form
Enter:
- Your Name
- WhatsApp Phone Number
- Contribution Amount (₹)
- Optional Prayer / Message Note
- Check "Anonymous" if you prefer to hide your name on the public wall.

### Step 4: Complete Payment via Razorpay
Select your preferred payment method (UPI / GPay / PhonePe / Card / NetBanking) and click **Proceed to Pay**.

### Step 5: Receive Instant WhatsApp Receipt
Upon payment completion:
1. An official receipt PDF link will be displayed on screen.
2. An automated WhatsApp message will be sent to your phone with your Receipt Number (e.g. `GAN-2026-000245`).
3. A second WhatsApp message will invite you to join the Devotee Community for daily pooja photos, live aarti videos, and immersion updates.

---

## 🔍 How to Download Past Receipts (No Account Required)

If you need to retrieve past receipts or view your contribution history:
1. Go to **Donor History** (`/donor/history`) or click "Donor History" in the navbar.
2. Enter your WhatsApp phone number.
3. Enter the 4-digit OTP sent to your WhatsApp (Demo OTP: `1234`).
4. Instantly view all your past contributions and download signed PDF receipts!

---

## 🛡️ How to Verify Receipt Authenticity

Anyone can verify the authenticity of a digital or cash receipt:
1. Go to **Verify Receipt** (`/verify`).
2. Enter the receipt number (e.g. `GAN-2026-000245`) or scan the receipt QR code.
3. The system will display the official committee seal, donor name/anonymous status, amount, timestamp, and transaction ID.
